Scanning a card

Open the Scan tab and hold the card flat in good light. Cardbook reads the text on your iPhone and lets you check every field before saving.

Remembering where you met

Type the event under “At an event?” on the Scan tab. Every card you scan after that is tagged with it. Sort or search your contacts by event.

Subscriptions

The first three cards are free. To cancel or change Cardbook Pro, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, tap Restore purchases in the app’s Settings tab.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
