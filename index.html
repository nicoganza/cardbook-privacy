Cardbook: Business Card Scan

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Summary

Cardbook does not collect any data. The business cards you scan, their photos and everything you write about your contacts stay on your iPhone.

Card photos and text

Photos are taken with the iPhone camera or chosen from your library. The text on them is read on the device by Apple’s Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Contacts

When you choose to add a card to your iPhone Contacts, Cardbook opens Apple’s contact form, filled in with the card’s details. You check it and save it yourself. Cardbook does not read your existing contacts.

QR codes and NFC

QR codes and NFC tags are read on your iPhone. Cardbook writes to an NFC tag only when you ask it to, and only the details you choose.

Sharing and export

A contact is only shared when you share it, for example as a vCard, a QR code or a CSV file, with the app you pick. Your own card (My Card) stays on your iPhone until you share it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Analytics and tracking

Cardbook has no analytics, ads or tracking, and no account.

Deleting your data

Deleting a card in the app deletes its details and photos. Deleting the app removes everything. Contacts you added to iPhone Contacts stay there until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
